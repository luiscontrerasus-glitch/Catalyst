Sources: [official rules](https://csc-winter-sparkathon.devpost.com/rules), [submission details and judging](https://csc-winter-sparkathon.devpost.com/).

High school students ages 13–18; solo or up to four per team. Development period: October 29–November 29. Theme: technology supporting connection, cooperation, support, generosity, or shared experiences. Credit outside work, disclose significant pre-existing assets and AI assistance, and be able to explain the product. Appropriate permission is required for private/sensitive data.

**Deadline discrepancy:** the Devpost header shows November 30 at **12:00 AM PST**; the rules prose says **12:00 PM Pacific** November 30. Use the owner's **November 29** target; obtain organizer clarification before relying on the later time.

Submit project/problem/solution explanations, evidence of function, technology and AI-use lists, team names, and source/build/design files if available. A short demo video is encouraged. Judging: learning, design, creativity, functionality, impact. The prototype's working what-if flow and transparent reasoning target these criteria; judges may ask about implementation and contributions.
